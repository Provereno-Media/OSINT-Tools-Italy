# OSINT-Tools-Italy
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/03/Flag_of_Italy.svg"/>

A list of OSINT resources and tools that may be useful to you when conducting investigations related to Italy. Most of the resources and tools has versions in Italian, some also in English. Persons are identified by the codice fiscale (tax code), companies by the partita IVA / codice fiscale and the REA number. Public access to the register of beneficial owners (Registro dei titolari effettivi) has been suspended since the Council of State referred questions to the CJEU in October 2024; Legislative Decree 210/2025 and a draft decree of March 2026 provide for access by journalists and researchers only after verification of a legitimate interest, so check the current status before relying on it. Vehicle owner data are available only through a paid PRA search (visura) with SPID, CIE, or CNS login.

## Open Data portals
(Country Open Data, Stock Exchange, Courts)
- [dati.gov.it](https://www.dati.gov.it/) — national open data portal with datasets published by central and local administrations. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [IstatData (Italian National Institute of Statistics)](https://esploradati.istat.it/databrowser/#/en) — Istat's data warehouse of official statistics, with downloads and an API. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [Gazzetta Ufficiale (Official Gazette)](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/) — archive of the Official Gazette: laws, decrees and official notices, including the section for public contracts and judicial announcements. *Lang: IT.*
- [Normattiva](https://www.normattiva.it/) — official database of Italian legislation in force, with consolidated texts. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [Corte Suprema di Cassazione (Supreme Court of Cassation)](https://www.cortedicassazione.it/) — Italy's highest court of appeal: news, decisions and access to its case-law services. *Lang: IT.*
- [Giustizia Amministrativa (Administrative Justice)](https://www.giustizia-amministrativa.it/) — portal of the Council of State and the regional administrative courts (TAR): decisions and case search, including public-procurement disputes. *Lang: IT, EN, DE.*
- [OpenCivitas](https://www.opencivitas.it/portale/) — Ministry of Economy and Sogei portal to search, compare and assess local governments: standard needs, services and performance of municipalities and provinces. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [OpenBDAP (Public Finance Database)](https://openbdap.rgs.mef.gov.it/) — State General Accounting Department's portal for public-finance data: budgets and accounts of public administrations, with a registry of public entities. *Lang: IT.*
- [Borsa Italiana — Listino A-Z](https://www.borsaitaliana.it/borsa/azioni/listino-a-z.html) — list of shares listed on Euronext Milan, with company pages, prices and issuer documents. *Lang: IT, EN.*

## Legal Entities
- [Registro Imprese (Chambers of Commerce Business Register)](https://www.registroimprese.it/) — official register of all Italian companies run by InfoCamere: free company search with basic profile; full records (visura), financial statements, shareholder lists and searches of a person's current and past company roles are paid. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [Agenzia delle Entrate — Verifica partita IVA (VAT Number Check)](https://telematici.agenziaentrate.gov.it/VerificaPIVA/Scegli.do?parameter=verificaPiva) — Revenue Agency's free check of whether a VAT number is active, with the registered name and status. *Lang: IT, EN, DE.*
- [OpenCorporates — Italy](https://opencorporates.com/registers/120) — global corporate data aggregator with entries from the Italian business register. *Lang: EN.*
- [Tesari First Resort](https://tesari.ai/) — Tesari is an all-source intelligence platform designed for high-stakes environments, with applications ranging from trade compliance and supply chain illumination to financial crime investigations and complex litigation. It is a paid platform that offers a Tech for Good (Community Impact) Programme for OSINT practitioners and journalists. *Lang: EN.*

## Maps
(Cadastral Maps if available, GIS etc.)
- [Geoportale Cartografico Catastale (Revenue Agency Cadastral Map)](https://geoportale.cartografia.agenziaentrate.gov.it/) — free consultation of the cadastral map: about 300,000 sheets with over 85 million parcels and 18 million buildings; search a parcel by its cadastral identifier; CC BY 4.0 data. Owner data require a paid cadastral search (visura). *Lang: IT.*
- [OMI — Quotazioni immobiliari (Real Estate Market Observatory)](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/web/guest/schede/fabbricatiterreni/omi/banche-dati/quotazioni-immobiliari) — Revenue Agency's half-yearly price ranges for property by municipality and zone. *Lang: IT.*
- [Geoportale Nazionale (National Geoportal)](https://gn.mase.gov.it/portale/home) — Ministry of Environment's INSPIRE geoportal: metadata catalogue, map viewer and national map services. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [Geoportale Regione Lombardia](https://www.geoportale.regione.lombardia.it/) — example of a regional geoportal: Lombardy's map viewer and geodata; most regions run their own. *Lang: IT.*

## Vehicles
### Land
- [Il Portale dell'Automobilista — Verifica copertura RC (Motor Insurance Check)](https://www.ilportaledellautomobilista.it/web/portale-automobilista/ext/verifica-copertura-rc) — Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport's free check of whether a vehicle has valid third-party insurance, by plate and vehicle type. *Lang: IT.*
- [Il Portale dell'Automobilista — Revisioni (Roadworthiness Tests)](https://www.ilportaledellautomobilista.it/web/portale-automobilista/veicoli/revisioni) — last inspection date, result and recorded mileage by plate (inspections since 1 June 2018). *Lang: IT.*
- [Platesmania — Italy](https://platesmania.com/it/search) — crowdsourced photos of vehicles with Italian plates; search by plate. *Lang: IT, EN.*

### Maritime
- [VesselFinder](https://www.vesselfinder.com/) — live AIS vessel map with vessel and port database. Freemium: live map free; some features paid. *Lang: EN.*
- [Equasis](https://www.equasis.org/) — international database of ship and shipping-company safety information: IMO number, flag, owner, manager, inspections. Free registration required. *Lang: EN.*

### Aviation
- [ENAC — Registro Aeromobili (Aircraft Register)](https://www.enac.gov.it/sicurezza-aerea/aeronavigabilita-iniziale/registro-aeromobili) — Italian Civil Aviation Authority's pages on the national aircraft register (RAN): searches (visure) and certificates on request, list of deregistered aircraft and notices. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://globe.adsbexchange.com/) — live and historical aircraft tracking without filtering of blocked aircraft; search by registration, ICAO hex or callsign. *Lang: EN.*

## People
(Names, Addresses, Yellow Pages, White Pages etc.)
- [Anagrafe degli amministratori locali e regionali (Register of Local and Regional Officials)](https://dait.interno.gov.it/elezioni/anagrafe-amministratori) — Ministry of the Interior's register of elected officials in municipalities, provinces, metropolitan cities and regions: personal data, list or group, office held. *Lang: IT.*
- [Camera dei deputati — Elenco deputati (Chamber of Deputies — Members)](https://www.camera.it/deputati/elenco) — deputies by legislature, with profiles, voting participation and asset declarations (dichiarazioni patrimoniali). *Lang: IT.*
- [Dati Camera (Chamber of Deputies Open Data)](https://dati.camera.it/) — open data on deputies, bills, votes and parliamentary acts. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [Openparlamento (Openpolis)](https://parlamento19.openpolis.it/) — Openpolis monitoring of government and MPs: votes, legislative activity and a power index for each parliamentarian. *Lang: IT.*
- [Eligendo — Archivio storico elettorale (Historical Election Archive)](https://elezionistorico.interno.gov.it/) — Ministry of the Interior's election results since 1948 by election type, area and candidate. *Lang: IT.*
- [FNOMCeO — Albo Unico Nazionale (Register of Doctors and Dentists)](https://albounico.fnomceo.it/) — national register of physicians and dentists; full surname required. *Lang: IT.*
- [Consiglio Nazionale Forense — Ricerca avvocati (National Bar Council — Lawyer Search)](https://www.consiglionazionaleforense.it/web/cnf/ricerca-avvocati) — search of lawyers registered with Italian bar associations. *Lang: IT.*
- [PagineBianche — Persone (White Pages)](https://www.paginebianche.it/persone) — telephone directory of private persons by name and town. Blocks automated access. *Lang: IT.*
- Try [OSINT Industries](https://app.osint.industries) and don't forget about the [Digital Profiler's Toolkit](https://github.com/paulpogoda/Digital-Profiler-s-Toolkit)

## Public procurement
- [ANAC — Portale dei dati aperti (Anti-Corruption Authority Open Data)](https://dati.anticorruzione.it/opendata) — open data from the National Database of Public Contracts (BDNCP): tenders, CIG codes, awards and contracting authorities, plus analytics dashboards. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [ANAC — Piattaforma di Pubblicità a Valore Legale (Legal Publication Platform)](https://pubblicitalegale.anticorruzione.it/) — official national publication of tender notices and awards since 2024. *Lang: IT.*
- [Acquisti in Rete PA (Consip)](https://www.acquistinretepa.it/opencms/opencms/) — e-procurement platform of the public administration run by Consip: framework agreements, marketplace and tenders. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [TED — Tenders Electronic Daily](https://ted.europa.eu/en/) — EU procurement-notice search; filter by country Italy for above-threshold notices and contract awards. *Lang: EN, multi.*

## WHOIS
- [Registro .it — Web Whois](https://www.nic.it/en/find-your-it/web-whois) — official .it registry WHOIS lookup. *Lang: IT, EN.*
- [IANA — .it Delegation Record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/it.html) — official delegation data for .it: registry operator, contacts and WHOIS server `whois.nic.it`. *Lang: EN.*
- [RIPE Database Query](https://apps.db.ripe.net/db-web-ui/query) — RIPE NCC WHOIS for IP ranges and AS numbers allocated to Italian networks. *Lang: EN.*
- [ViewDNS.info](https://viewdns.info/) — reverse IP, reverse WHOIS, IP history, DNS records and subdomain discovery. *Lang: EN.*
